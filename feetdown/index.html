Feet Down

Mission alarm. 60 steps and it goes quiet.

Feet Down is an alarm clock for iPhone that keeps ringing until you have done a mission: walk 60 steps, solve sums, photograph a spot, scan a barcode or type a line. Free forever: one alarm with a Steps or Maths mission.

Help

The alarm did not ring

Feet Down rings through Apple's AlarmKit, like the Clock app. Open Settings › Apps › Feet Down and make sure Alarms is allowed. If you turned it down during setup, Feet Down shows a screen with a link to that setting. Alarms ring through Silent mode and Focus; the volume follows the ringer volume.

I pressed Stop and it rang again

That is the Backstop. Stop on the Lock Screen does not end the alarm: it rings again every 2 minutes (for up to 10 minutes free, 20 or 30 with Pro) until you finish the mission in the app. Tap Start mission instead of Stop.

My steps are not counted

Steps come from the iPhone's motion sensor. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness and turn on Feet Down. Without Motion & Fitness, a Steps mission switches to sums, and the alarm still stops only when you finish. Keep the iPhone in your hand or pocket while you walk.

How do I test it?

Set an alarm one minute ahead, lock the iPhone and wait. Or open the alarm and tap Practice this mission now.

Skip tomorrow's alarm

Use Skip next only on the alarm, or the Control Centre control or the Shortcut "Skip next alarm". Skipping is refused within 30 minutes of the ring.

I bought Feet Down Pro but it isn't active

Open Feet Down › Settings › Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the free trial ends and you are not charged. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
