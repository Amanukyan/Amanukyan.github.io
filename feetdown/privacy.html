Feet Down Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Your alarms, steps, photos and barcodes stay on your iPhone. Feet Down has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no server of its own.

Alarms (AlarmKit)

Feet Down schedules your alarms with Apple's AlarmKit so they ring through Silent mode and Focus. Your alarm times, missions, mornings and streak are saved on your device (shared with the Feet Down widgets through an App Group on the same iPhone). Deleting the app deletes them.

Steps (Motion & Fitness)

During a Steps mission, Feet Down reads the step count from the iPhone's motion sensor to know when to stop the alarm. Only the count for that mission is used. It is not uploaded, and Feet Down does not read Apple Health.

Camera (Photo and Scan missions)

For a Photo mission you take 3 reference shots of one spot; in the morning, the new photo is compared with them on the device using Apple's Vision framework. For a Scan mission, the barcode you choose is read on the device with Apple's VisionKit and only its number is saved. Photos and barcodes never leave your iPhone.

Notifications

Notifications are local, scheduled on your iPhone: the "Still up?" question after an alarm and a reminder the day before a free trial ends. Nothing is sent through a push server.

Purchases

Feet Down Pro is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and nothing about your alarms, steps or photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Feet Down collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
