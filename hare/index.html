Hare

Type the time you want. Chase the hare.

Hare turns your goal time into a pacer you chase through all 16 parts of a hybrid fitness race (8 runs, 8 stations), then shows where you beat it and where it got away. Your first race is free from card to poster, with the Watch pacer, rehearsals and widgets.

Help

The card is on my iPhone but not on my watch

Install Hare on the Apple Watch (Watch app on the iPhone › Hare › Show App on Apple Watch), then open Hare on the iPhone once with the watch nearby. Cards travel to the watch on their own and stay there with no phone and no signal.

I missed a press during the race

The hare runs on the clock, so one missed press never stops the race. Press at the next change: Hare sees that a part was skipped and fills it in. Hold the screen to step back if you pressed by accident.

The workout isn't in Health

Open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Hare and allow Workouts. Hare still paces without Health; it just can't save the workout.

The screenshot reader missed some times

Hare reads labels like Run 3 or Sled Pull and the time on the same row. Anything it can't read is left open on the keypad, marked, so you can type it.

I bought the unlock but it isn't active

Open Hare › the "i" on the card › Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
