Hare Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Your race cards, splits, rehearsals and posters stay on your iPhone and your Apple Watch. Hare has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no server of its own. Nothing you type or race is sent to us.

Race cards and splits

Your goal times, race dates, cards, past-race splits and rehearsal results are saved on your device (shared with the Hare widgets through an App Group on the same iPhone, and sent to your own Apple Watch with Apple's WatchConnectivity). Deleting the app deletes them.

Screenshots of results

If you choose a screenshot of a past result, Hare reads the times from it on the device with Apple's Vision framework. Hare sees only the image you pick, never your whole library, and does not keep or upload it.

Photos

When you save a poster or a Lock Screen card, Hare asks to add it to Photos. It can only add; it cannot read your library.

Apple Health and the Watch

During a race or rehearsal, the Hare Watch app runs a workout. If you allow it, it reads your heart rate and active energy while that workout runs and saves the workout to Apple Health as one mixed cardio workout with its parts marked. Hare reads no other Health data, and Health data never leaves your devices.

Notifications

Any reminders are local, scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent through a push server.

Usage counts

To see which steps people reach (card kept, poster made, and so on), Hare counts them on your iPhone only. The counts have no identifiers and no health data, and they are never sent off the device.

Purchases

The unlock is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have bought it. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and nothing about your races, splits or health. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Not affiliated

Hare is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by HYROX.

Children

Hare collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
