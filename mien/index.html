Mien

Face yoga, shown not told.

Mien is a face yoga coach for iPhone. A line-drawn face demonstrates every move, a calm voice names each step, and you copy and hold. Three, six or ten minutes a day, in a 28-day program built from a few questions.

Help

The mirror is greyed out

The mirror uses Face ID's TrueDepth camera, so it needs an iPhone with Face ID. Sessions work the same without it.

The mirror says camera access is off

Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera and turn on Mien. The camera image is never recorded or uploaded.

I don't hear the voice

Check that the ring/silent switch is not on silent and the volume is up, then open Mien › Settings › Sound and touch › Voice guidance.

My reminder doesn't arrive

Open Settings › Notifications › Mien and allow notifications, then set the time in Mien › Settings › Reminder.

Where are my check-in photos?

Inside Mien on your iPhone only, under Progress. They are not in your photo library and not in any cloud, so deleting the app deletes them.

I subscribed but Mien Plus isn't active

Open the plans screen and tap Restore while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
