Mien Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Your face, your photos and your answers never leave your iPhone. Mien has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The camera and the mirror

The mirror is optional. When you turn it on, Mien uses the front camera and Apple's ARKit face tracking to see how still you hold a move. This happens on your iPhone, frame by frame: no video or image is recorded, stored or uploaded, and no face data is sent to us or anyone else. Mien keeps only a simple score per session (how steady the holds were), stored on your device.

Check-in photos

If you take check-in photos, they are saved inside Mien on your iPhone. They are never uploaded, never shared and never used for anything but showing them back to you. Delete a photo in Progress, or erase everything with Settings › Start over. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Your answers and your history

Your goal, the areas you chose, your session length, reminder time, streak and session history are stored on your device to build your program. They are not sent anywhere.

Reminders

With your permission, Mien schedules local notifications on your iPhone (a daily reminder, and a reminder before a free trial ends). No server is involved.

Purchases

Mien Plus is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and nothing about your face, photos or answers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Mien is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
