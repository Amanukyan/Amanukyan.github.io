Outtake

Left is out. Right stays.

Outtake is a swipe photo cleaner for iPhone. Go through your camera roll one month at a time: swipe left to mark a photo out, right to keep it.

Help

I swiped left. Is the photo gone?

No. Photos you swipe left wait in the tray at the bottom left. Nothing is deleted until you open the tray and press Delete, and iOS asks you to confirm.

I deleted photos but my storage did not change

iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days, then frees the space. To free it now, open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted and delete them there.

I want a photo back

Before you press Delete, tap the photo in the tray to keep it. After deleting, open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted and tap Recover (within 30 days).

Outtake only sees some of my photos

You gave Outtake access to selected photos only. Open Settings › Apps › Outtake › Photos and choose Full Access.

I bought Outtake Pro but it isn't active

Open the menu in Outtake › Settings › Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
