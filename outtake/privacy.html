Outtake Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Your photos never leave your iPhone. Outtake has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your photo library

Outtake reads your photo library on your device so you can sort it. Photos, videos, their dates, places and file sizes are read on the device only and are never uploaded. Which photos you marked keep or out is saved in a small file on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos are only deleted when you press Delete in the tray and confirm in the iOS prompt. They then go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

Place names

To show a city name under a photo, Outtake asks Apple Maps to turn the photo's coordinates into a place name. Apple handles this request under its own privacy policy. No photo is sent.

Purchases

Outtake Pro is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription or purchase is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and nothing about your photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Outtake collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
