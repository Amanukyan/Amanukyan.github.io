Shadowbook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

Shadowbook collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server of its own. Listening happens on your iPhone and no audio is sent anywhere. Family recordings stay on your iPhone and leave it only when you send one yourself.

The microphone and speech recognition

If you allow it, Shadowbook listens while you read aloud so the puppets move on your words, and listens for your child's shout, breath or whisper at their turn in the story. Speech recognition runs on the iPhone (Apple's on-device recognition): nothing you say is sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else, and the sound is not recorded or kept. For a shout or a breath, Shadowbook measures only how loud the room is. You can say no, or switch listening off in Settings; tapping always works.

Family voices

When someone records a reading in Voices, the recording is saved on that iPhone, in Shadowbook's own storage. It leaves the iPhone only if that person chooses Send to family and sends the file themselves, with the app they pick (Messages, Mail, AirDrop and so on). It goes from their device to yours; it never passes through us, and we never see it. Deleting a reading, or the app, deletes the recording.

What stays on your iPhone

The age band you choose (used only to order the library), your settings, your reading nights and your bedtime reminder time are saved on your iPhone. The widget reads tonight's story from the same iPhone through an App Group. None of it is sent anywhere.

Notifications

The bedtime reminder and the note two days before a free trial ends are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing goes through a push server.

Purchases

The subscription is bought and managed through Apple's App Store. Shadowbook checks it with Apple's StoreKit on the device and uses no third-party purchase or analytics service. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Shadowbook is made for families with young children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included, shows no ads and contains no third-party code. Purchases, settings, recording and every link that leaves the app sit behind a grown-up question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
