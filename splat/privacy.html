Splat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version. Splat collects nothing. There is no account, no ads, no analytics service and no server. What you log about your baby stays on your iPhone, and we never see it.

What you log

The foods you log, how each one went, the dates, your baby's name and birthday if you add them, reaction notes and the allergen plan are stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone (shared with Splat's widgets on the same phone). They are included in your iPhone or iCloud backup the way all app data is, under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Photos

Splat only sees a photo when you choose one to attach to a reaction note, through the system photo picker. That copy is kept inside the app on your iPhone. Splat never reads the rest of your photo library. If you save the poster or the time-lapse to Photos, iOS asks you once to let Splat add to your library; that permission only lets it add, not look.

Notifications

The Monday reminder and allergen plan reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent through a server.

Sharing

The poster, the time-lapse video, the PDF and the tray file for a second caregiver are made on your iPhone. They go only where you send them (AirDrop, Messages, email, Files), and we never receive a copy.

Usage counts

The app keeps a few anonymous counters on your iPhone, such as whether the first splat landed, whether the unlock screen was seen or the poster was saved. They contain no food names, notes, photos, names or identifiers, they stay on the device and they are never sent anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Apple and checked with Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone. Apple processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Splat is used by parents and caregivers to keep a record about their child. The developer collects no data about the child or anyone else. Nothing you enter leaves your iPhone unless you send it yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
