Thrown Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Your Health and motion data never leave your iPhone. Thrown has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Health and motion data

With your permission, Thrown reads your step count and walking and running distance from Apple Health, and step counts from the iPhone's motion sensor. It uses them on your device to draw a pot for each day. These data are never uploaded, never shared with anyone and never used for advertising or marketing. Thrown writes nothing to Health.

The pots, your daily goal, the notes you add to a day and your settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you share a pot or the year poster, Thrown makes the image on your device. It contains only what you see on the card, and it goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

Thrown Studio is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription or purchase is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and nothing about your health or steps. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Thrown collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
