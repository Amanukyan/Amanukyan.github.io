Tumble Block

Roll it. Stand it. Solve it.

Tumble Block is a calm 3D logic puzzle for iPhone. Roll the block across floating tiles and stand it upright on the glowing goal.

Help

How do I play?

Swipe to roll the block one step. Stand it upright on the glowing tile to finish the level. Glass tiles hold the block lying down but shatter if it stands on them. Round buttons switch bridges on and off; square plates only work when the block stands on them.

I made a wrong move

Tap Undo to roll back one move at a time. Falling off never costs you the level: the block is put back where it was.

I'm stuck

Tap Hint to see the best next move. You start with a few free hints and can earn more by watching a short video. Premium gives unlimited hints.

I bought Premium but it isn't active

Open Settings › Restore Purchase in the game while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.
