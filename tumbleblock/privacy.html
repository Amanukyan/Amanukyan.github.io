Tumble Block Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Tumble Block ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Aleksandr Manukyan, an independent developer. This policy explains what information is involved when you play it.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (levels reached, stars, hints, settings) is stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and I don't receive this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when asked), IP address and approximate location derived from it, information about your device, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy Choices. On iPhone, you can also turn off tracking under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you buy Premium, no ads are shown.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. I never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

To check whether you own Premium and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app (what was bought and when). It does not receive your name, email address or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed specifically at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because the app itself keeps your data on your device, deleting the app removes it. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, or for any other question, contact me at the address below and I will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
