Two Skies

Your Human Design chart, drawn from the sky you were born under, and explained in plain English: how you work, decide and rest.

Your chart, type, strategy, authority, profile and all nine centres are free. "The whole design" (one payment, or yearly) adds every channel, gate and line written for you, your cross, unlimited people and your daily sky.

Support

My birth time is unknown. Choose "I don't know the time"; the chart uses midday. Your type is usually still right, but the Moon and sometimes your profile may differ. You can add the time later in Settings.

My chart looks different from another app. Check the UTC offset on the place screen. Time-zone records before 1970 are wrong for some places; if you know the clocks were different where you were born, edit the offset.

Restore a purchase. Open the paywall or Settings and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Cancel the yearly plan. Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.
