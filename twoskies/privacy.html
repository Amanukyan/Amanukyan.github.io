Two Skies Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Your birth details never leave your iPhone. Two Skies has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your chart is calculated on the device.

Birth details and charts

The birth date, time and place you enter, and those of anyone you add, are stored only in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Every chart, reading and daily sky is calculated on the device, offline. Deleting the app, or using "Delete everything" in its settings, removes all of it.

Finding a birth place

When you type a town, Two Skies asks Apple Maps to find it and its time zone, the same as searching in the Maps app. Apple handles this request under its own privacy policy. Nothing else about you is sent with it, and we never see it.

Sharing

If you share your chart poster, your reading as a PDF, or an invitation link, it goes only where you send it. An invitation link carries the birth details you chose to put in it, inside the link itself; it passes through no server of ours.

Usage counts

The app keeps simple counts on your iPhone (for example, how many times the reading was opened) to help us understand which parts are used. They stay on the device and are never sent anywhere.

Purchases

"The whole design" is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase or subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and nothing about your birth details or chart. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

Two Skies collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
